- Recommendation: option 2, text wordmarks only in brand colors (or option 3 generic badges). Do not embed official logo images.

## Logo and trademark findings

### Caterpillar Inc.

- Guideline page: https://www.cat.com/en_GB/copyright.html (also en_US, en_IN variants). All three fetches timed out. The following is from a WebSearch result snippet, NOT a fetched page, so verify before citing: "CAT, CATERPILLAR, their respective logos, 'Caterpillar Yellow,' and the POWER EDGE trade dress as well as corporate and product identity used herein, are trademarks of Caterpillar and may not be used without permission." and "Nothing contained herein shall be construed as conferring any license or right under any Caterpillar copyright, patent or trademark unless expressly authorized in writing by Caterpillar."
- Supplier guidance (search snippet only, https://www.caterpillar.com/en/company/working-together/suppliers/supplier-communication.html): use of Caterpillar logo on supplier websites is "rarely permitted" and needs additional approval. Not directly applicable (we are not a supplier) but shows the posture.
- No public brand-asset/press-kit page found. No non-commercial, educational, or nominative-use language found.
- Asset: Wikimedia Commons https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Caterpillar_logo.svg (fetched). Tags: "This logo image consists only of simple geometric shapes or text. It does not meet the threshold of originality needed for copyright protection, and is therefore in the public domain." plus "This work includes material that may be protected as a trademark in some jurisdictions. If you want to use it, you have to ensure that you have the legal right to do so and that you do not infringe any trademark rights."
- Wordmark: "CAT" (logo) / "Caterpillar" (company). Wikipedia: "trademark 'Caterpillar Yellow' livery and the 'CAT' logo".
- Colors: no official hex published on a Caterpillar page I could fetch. Third-party BrandPalettes (fetched, claims "confirmed by the Caterpillar company"): yellow #FFC300, black #2E2725. A search result gave #FFCD00 (PMS 1235 C), unverified. Treat both as unofficial.
- Verdict: guidelines do not permit an unaffiliated project to display the logo image as far as found. Copyright is not the barrier (Commons PD), trademark is.

Quotes: "You may only use INNIO's trademarks with our permission, including as authorized by any brand guidelines we publish." / "INNIO may revoke permission to use INNIO's trademarks at any time." / "The absence of a trademark or logo from this list does not constitute a waiver of INNIO's trademark or other intellectual property rights concerning that trademark or logo." Registered marks include WAUKESHA (stylized), INNIO, JENBACHER, VHP, SERIES FOUR, ENGINATOR. Contact via https://www.innio.com/en/contact.
- Page text as returned did not mention non-commercial, educational, or nominative use.
- Asset: no official press-kit logo download found (https://www.innio.com/en/press returned 404; media center exists but no logo pack found). No Commons category/file found (Category:Waukesha_Engine 404).
- Wordmark: "WAUKESHA" (stylized) and "INNIO". Colors: not published in anything fetched.
- Verdict: permission required; no carve-out found. Do not display logo images.

### Recommendation

Option 2: render plain text "CATERPILLAR" / "CAT" and "WAUKESHA" as nominative references (naming the engine make, which is how PROPWR itself does it: "G3520", "Waukesha 9394S5"), styled in a neutral UI font. Brand-color styling is a minor risk since Caterpillar Yellow is itself claimed as a trademark; safest is neutral colors, or option 3 generic badges. Add a footer disclaimer: independent demo, not affiliated with or endorsed by PROPWR, Caterpillar, or INNIO; all marks belong to their owners. This is a risk judgment from the quoted text, not legal advice.
